# Agnes image-to-video guidance

Treat the source image as the visible opening state. Describe what moves, what remains stable, and one readable endpoint. Preserve identity, wardrobe, product geometry, and scene layout only where the brief requires them. Use one camera move and motivated lighting. Audio is native and always generated, but do not invent dialogue, music, or sound effects.
