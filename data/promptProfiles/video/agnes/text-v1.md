# Agnes text-to-video guidance

Describe a concrete subject and one legible action path before visual style. Give the camera one motivated move, name a physical light source, and keep scene density proportional to the identity and motion requirements. Audio is native and always generated, but add sound intent only when the brief supplies it; never invent dialogue, music, or sound effects.
