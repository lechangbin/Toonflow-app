# Seedance 2.0 text-to-video guidance

Spend the prompt budget on one primary objective: identity, motion, or scene density. Use visible actions instead of abstract emotion, one motivated camera move, physically sourced light, and explicit sound intent when supplied. Do not add future story beats or unsupported reference syntax.
